Sugarcane dataset attribution and license notice

The analysis-facing tables in this directory are adapted from the public dataset:

Gilles Chaix, Ryad Bendoula, and Abdallah Zgouz,
"Data set of Visible-Near Infrared handled and micro-spectrometers - comparison of their accuracy
for predicting some sugarcane properties," Mendeley Data, Version 1 (2021).
https://doi.org/10.17632/mjttsjfj2s.1

Related publication:

Abdallah Zgouz et al., "Dataset of visible-near infrared handheld and micro-spectrometers -
comparison of the prediction accuracy of sugarcane properties," Data in Brief 31 (2020), 106013.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2020.106013

Mendeley Data identifies the dataset license as Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Under CC BY 4.0, the data may be shared and adapted for any purpose provided appropriate credit is
given, a link to the license is supplied, and modifications are indicated.

Changes made for this repository:

- selected the LabSpec instrument table as the predictor source;
- parsed TS, CP, ADF, and IVOMD as responses;
- excluded source samples 103, 105, and 111 because TS is missing;
- aligned retained rows one-to-one using the public Sample identifier;
- retained wavelengths from 780 through 2500 nm inclusive;
- removed Sample identifiers from the numeric model matrices;
- wrote the aligned values as comma-delimited X.csv and Y.csv;
- added public metadata and integrity hashes.

No imputation, smoothing, derivative, scatter correction, centering, scaling, or other spectral
preprocessing was applied. No endorsement by the original authors or repository is implied.
